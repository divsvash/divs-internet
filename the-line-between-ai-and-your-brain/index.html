The line between AI and your brain

BY DIVS · 09.09.2026 · 5 MIN READ

With the vibe coding boom, I think it’s pretty easy to say that having thoughts and creativity is dead.

It’s not dead. We’ve just become extremely good at outsourcing it.

Creativity and thoughts aren’t a magical substance that disappears the moment you use a tool. But there is a line between using AI to sharpen an idea and asking it to manufacture the idea, the taste, and the reason for making anything at all.

The tool can finish the sentence. It cannot decide why the sentence deserved to exist.

The part worth protecting

The most valuable part of building isn’t typing every character yourself. It is noticing the problem, choosing what matters, rejecting the obvious answer, and caring enough to keep going when the first version is bad.

AI can speed up execution. It can expose gaps. It can help you move beyond what you already know. But if every decision is delegated, the result may be technically complete and still feel like nobody was there.

I don’t think the answer is to reject AI. I think the answer is to remain present. Keep your taste. Keep your friction. Know which thoughts are yours before you ask a machine to improve them.
